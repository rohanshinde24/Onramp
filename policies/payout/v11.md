## 1.1 Scope

This policy governs payment for tasks completed by workers on the marketplace, including
authorization, settlement, and corrections when a payment does not arrive.

## 2.1 Eligibility

A worker is owed the task payout when their submission has been accepted and has passed
quality review. A submission that was rejected, or whose quality review failed and was not
overturned, is not eligible for payment.

## 3.1 Amount owed

The amount owed is the payout listed on the task at the time the worker was assigned to it.
Later changes to the task listing do not change the amount owed for existing assignments.

## 4.1 Authorization

Payment for an eligible submission is authorized within one day of acceptance.

## 4.2 Settlement window

Settlement of an authorized payment is expected within 5 calendar days of authorization.
Before the window has passed, a payment that has not settled is pending, not missing, and no
correction is made.

## 4.3 Missing settlement

When the settlement window has passed and no settlement has succeeded for an eligible
submission, the payment is missing. The full amount owed is paid to the worker as a payout
adjustment.

## 5.1 Failed settlement

A settlement that fails, for example because the bank rejected it, does not count as payment.
The worker remains owed the full amount, which is paid as a payout adjustment once the window
has passed.

## 6.1 Duplicate payments

If more than one payment exists for the same submission, no adjustment is made. The case is
referred to a lead for finance review, because paying again could pay the worker twice.

## 7.1 Adjustment limits

A payout adjustment above the operator's approval limit is not issued from a support case and
is escalated to a lead.
