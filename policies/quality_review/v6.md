## 1.1 Scope

This policy governs how submissions are reviewed and how workers may dispute a review.

## 2.1 Applicable criteria

A reviewer scores only the criteria that the task instructions ask for. A criterion that does
not apply to the task is marked not applicable and is excluded from the overall score.

## 3.1 Passing score

A submission passes review when its overall score, averaged over the applicable criteria, is
at least 0.75. The threshold was raised from 0.70.

## 4.1 Overturning a review

A review is overturned when it scored a criterion that did not apply to the task, or when the
overall score over the applicable criteria meets the passing score. An overturned review
counts as passed, and the submission becomes payable.

## 5.1 Dispute window

A worker may dispute a review within 14 days of the review. The window was extended from 7
days. Disputes filed later are not reconsidered.

## 6.1 Unclear criteria

If the task instructions are ambiguous about whether a criterion applies, the dispute is
referred to a lead.
