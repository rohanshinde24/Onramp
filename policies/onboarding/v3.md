## 1.1 Scope

This policy sets what a worker must complete before their account is activated.

## 2.1 Required verifications

A worker is activated once both an identity verification and a skills assessment have
passed.

## 2.2 Tax documentation

Tax documentation is collected at the first payout and is not required for activation.

## 3.1 Identity verification validity

An identity verification is valid for 365 days after it was completed. An expired
verification must be repeated before activation.

## 4.1 Failed verification

A failed identity verification is final for this application. A failed skills assessment may
be retaken after 30 days; until then the worker is not activated.

## 5.1 Mismatched identity

If the name on a verification does not match the worker's account, the application is
referred to a lead. It is not activated or denied from a support case.
